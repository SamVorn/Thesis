Institutional/government IDs → always STRICT regardless of context
Special categories (political, sexual orientation, gender, immigration) → STRICT due to harm risk not just re-ID risk
Financial figures → STRICT if exact (net worth), MODERATE if generalizable (income bracket, portfolio size)
Third-party names → STRICT, no exceptions
Broad demographics and self-reported statuses → RELAXED
Minors in particular get elevated protection


Stigma/discrimination risk → STRICT (mental health, HIV, substance use, reproductive)
Serious disease + treatment history → STRICT (cancer, cardiovascular, COPD, chemo)
Common/lifestyle conditions → MODERATE or RELAXED depending on specificity
Subjective self-ratings and opinions → RELAXED
